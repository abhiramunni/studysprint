How does the streak work?
Your streak grows by one day when you finish at least one study task or complete one focus session on a calendar day. Miss a day and it restarts — small daily wins beat occasional marathons.

A note for parents

StudySprint is built so the whole family understands it.

  • Day streak = how many days in a row your child studied. Celebrate the streak, not just marks.
  • Chapters done = syllabus progress, chapter by chapter. Ask "what did you tick off today?"
  • Focus hours = deep, timed study sessions (25 minutes each by default).
  • Revision queue = topics your child flagged as shaky — perfect for a quick quiz together.
  • All data stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
